Types of Fonts

Fonts come in many different styles, each creating a different mood and purpose in design. Below are some of the most common categories of fonts with examples and download links.

1. Serif Fonts

Serif fonts have small decorative lines, or “serifs,” at the ends of letters. They are often seen as traditional and professional, making them popular in print and formal design.

Example: Times New Roman

Download Merriweather

2. Sans-Serif Fonts

Sans-serif fonts don’t have the extra decorative strokes at the ends of letters. They look clean and modern, which makes them popular for digital content and minimalist designs.

Example: Arial

Download Open Sans

3. Script Fonts

Script fonts mimic handwriting and calligraphy. They are often elegant or decorative and are used for invitations, logos, or titles rather than long text.

Example: Brush Script MT

Download Great Vibes

4. Display Fonts

Display fonts are creative and eye-catching. They’re best for large headings, posters, and branding because they stand out visually but can be hard to read in long paragraphs.

Example: Impact

Download Bebas Neue

5. Monospace Fonts

Monospace fonts have equal spacing between each character. They’re commonly used in programming and technical documents because they make code easier to read.

Example: Courier New

Download Roboto Mono
