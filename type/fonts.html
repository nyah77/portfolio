Types of Fonts

Fonts come in many styles, each creating a different mood and purpose in design. Below are common font categories with examples and links to download.

1. Serif Fonts

Serif fonts have small decorative lines, or “serifs,” at the ends of letters. They are often seen as traditional and professional.

Example: Times New Roman

Download Merriweather

2. Sans-Serif Fonts

Sans-serif fonts don’t have the extra decorative strokes. They look clean and modern, popular for digital and minimalist designs.

Example: Arial

Download Open Sans

3. Script Fonts

Script fonts mimic handwriting and calligraphy. Often elegant, used for invitations, logos, or titles.

Example: Brush Script MT

Download Great Vibes

4. Display Fonts

Display fonts are creative and eye-catching. Best for large headings, posters, and branding.

Example: Impact

Download Bebas Neue

5. Monospace Fonts

Monospace fonts have equal spacing between each character. Often used in programming or technical documents.

Example: Courier New

Download Roboto Mono
